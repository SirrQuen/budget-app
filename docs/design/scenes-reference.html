Round 3 · A scene for every part of the day

The greeting lives in a little landscape

The dashboard banner becomes a small painted scene: sky, sun or moon, layered hills, mist, clouds or stars, with the roselle and the day's visitor standing in it. Four scenes, each drawn twice, deep for the dark theme and airy for the light theme, so the greeting always reads clearly. As much scenery as fits: distant mountains, two layers of hills, trees, mist, birds, clouds or stars, with the sky rising behind the greeting. The scene has no hard edge: it melts into the page background at the bottom and sides, so the dashboard's cards (the strip under each scene) sit on the same ground.

Always readableThe sky stays calm behind the words, and a soft fade from the left keeps the greeting at 4.5:1 or better in every scene and both themes. Detail lives on the right.
Still, not busyNo drifting clouds or twinkling loops. When the part of the day changes, the scene cross-fades once (about 600ms). Reduced motion: it just appears.
One home for the plant and visitorThe roselle stands on the near hill and the visitor perches on it, so they belong to the scene rather than floating over the header.
Melts into the pageNo box and no seam: the sky and hills fade into the page's own background colour at the bottom and both sides, in each theme. Drawn in code, no images, same hours as the Step 8 glow it replaces.
