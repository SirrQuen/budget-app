Round 3 · The sorrel, alive

Roselle, the Jamaican sorrel

Pale, ruffled petals around a crimson eye and a gold centre, beside the glossy deep-red calyx, the "bulb" that makes the sorrel drink. Roselle flowers open with the morning and twist shut later in the day, while the red calyx stays. So the dashboard plant opens and closes with the user's day, and stays recognisably sorrel even when closed.

Morning · opening
The leaves lift, the petals spiral open, and the crimson eye appears.
Evening · closing
The petals twist shut into a pale bud and the leaves relax. The red calyx stays.
True to the plantPale cream petals with a blush at the base, a crimson eye and a gold centre. Glossy deep-red calyces with pointed lobes and a spiky collar, maroon stems, and lobed green leaves with red veins.
When it movesOnce, on the first dashboard visit after its clock changes: opening from 7am, closing from 7pm, on the same clock as today. Otherwise it rests open or closed. Motion means something changed.
In turn with everything elseWelcome bloom first, then the sorrel opens or closes (about 1.6s), then the visitor arrives. Never two at once.
The logo stays the logoThe three-leaflet mark keeps icons, sign-in and the brand. This is the dashboard's own living plant. It's an illustration, not data, so the reds here never describe money.
Calm versionWith reduced motion it simply shows open or closed for the time of day. Turn on Reduce motion in this board's Tweaks to see it.
