One scene, the whole screenThe scene is fixed behind the page. The top shows it clearly: sky, sun or moon, mountains, hills and the roselle. Below the hills the ground carries on to the bottom of the screen, calm and plain, so there is always landscape behind the cards.
Cards are frosted glassEvery tile and chart sits on a frosted card, about 78% opaque in dark and 82% in light, with a soft blur of what is behind it. Numbers, charts and bars keep their normal colours and read exactly as they do today.
A little blur as you scrollOnce the cards start covering the hills, the scene behind softens and dims slightly, like the weather app. It is tied to your scroll, never plays by itself, and stops when you stop.
The sidebar stays solidOn desktop the scene fills the main area. Navigation keeps its solid panel so it is always easy to find.
Reduced motion and transparencyReduced motion: the scene stays sharp and still while you scroll. Reduced transparency (or an old browser): cards turn solid, as they are today.
