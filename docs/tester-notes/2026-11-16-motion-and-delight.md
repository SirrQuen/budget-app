Sorrel now moves with you, a little. Nothing about your numbers changes.

- **Safe to spend rolls.** When you confirm a paycheck or add something and
  the figure changes, the digits roll to the new number. A rise gets a soft
  glow behind it; a fall just rolls. Opening Sorrel never animates it.
- **Saving feels like something.** After you add a transaction the button
  says Saved, a couple of leaves float up, and a note offers **Log again**.
  The first thing you log each day also gets a small "First one today".
- **Empty screens have a drawing** and a button to get going.
- **Your dashboard cards lift** when you drag them, and a dashed outline
  shows where they will land.
- Balances ease in after a save, pages fade in as you move between them,
  and loading placeholders pulse slowly.

If your device is set to reduce motion, all of this shows its end state at
once, with no movement.
