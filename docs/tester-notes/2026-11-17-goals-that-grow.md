Goals now grow.

- **Your goal is a stem with leaves.** The bar has a leaf at a quarter,
  halfway and three-quarters. A leaf fills in the moment your progress
  passes it.
- **It moves when something changes, not every time you open the page.**
  Add a contribution and the bar grows to the new spot. Come back after
  progress has changed and it grows from where you last saw it, once.
  Reload with nothing new and it just sits there. Passing a quarter, half
  or three-quarters also says so, in a short line.
- **A goal that follows a card can ease back** when the card's balance goes
  up. The bar shrinks quietly and the line still reads what's left to go.
- **Finishing a goal** still blooms once. After that the open flower sits
  beside "Reached on" the date, or "Paid off on" for a card.

If your device is set to reduce motion, the bar and leaves show where they
are, with no movement.
