You can now bring in a CSV file from your bank instead of typing each
transaction. Open an account and choose **Import**, or go to Settings and
choose **Import transactions**.

How it works:

- **The file stays on your device.** Sorrel reads it in your browser and
  never uploads it, never keeps it, and never keeps its name. Only the
  transactions you confirm are sent, when you press Import.
- **You see what it does first.** The preview says how many transactions
  are coming in, how much was spent and received, and what your account's
  balance changes from and to. Nothing is saved until you press Import.
- **A few answers are yours to give, and nothing is guessed.** Whether
  money you spent shows as a negative or a positive number in your file.
  Which way a date like 03/04/2026 reads, when the file doesn't say.
  Whether your balance already included the transactions dated before your
  balance date. Which category each unmatched name goes in.
- **Possible duplicates are skipped.** A transaction with the same amount
  within three days of one you already have is left out unless you tick
  Include.
- **Bills.** If a row looks like a bill your schedule is still waiting to
  hear about, Sorrel asks "Is this the same bill?". Yes counts it once and
  the schedule carries on from the next one.
- **Every import can be undone,** from Settings, in one step.

This changes your numbers whenever you import: your account balance, and so
Safe to Spend, move by what you bring in.

Sorrel's own CSV export (Settings, "Download transactions") can be brought
back in the same way.

**The privacy policy has changed.** It used to say everything financial in
Sorrel is information you typed in yourself. It now says you typed in or
imported it, that an imported file is read on your device and never
uploaded, and that Sorrel never connects to your bank. That is the only
change, and nothing changes about who can see your data.
