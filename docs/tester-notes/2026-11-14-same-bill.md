If you've typed in a bill ahead of time that also has a recurring
schedule, you may have seen it twice under Upcoming: once marked
"(fixed)", from the schedule, and once "(as entered)", from your entry.
Safe to Spend was counting it twice too, so it showed less than you
really have.

Sorrel now spots those pairs and asks: "Is this the same bill?"

- **Yes, they're the same** joins them. Your entry stays and counts
  once. The schedule skips that date and carries on from the next one,
  so you won't be asked to confirm it on the day. Safe to Spend may go
  up, because that bill is no longer counted twice.
- **No, keep both** leaves both, and Sorrel won't ask about that pair
  again. If you change your mind, open the entry and choose "Change your
  answer".

The question shows in Upcoming on the dashboard, on the entry itself,
and right after you save an entry like that.

If you later delete an entry you joined, the schedule takes that date
back, so the bill stays in your forecast. If the schedule has already
moved past it by then, the delete message tells you first.

Nothing changes until you answer.
