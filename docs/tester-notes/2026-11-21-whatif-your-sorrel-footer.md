Four small things.

**Your Sorrel.** Sorrel now notices a few moments in your time with it, and
keeps them. Open "Your Sorrel" beside the greeting on the dashboard (the
little leaf changes with the season), or from the menu beside your name. A
panel opens with the day you started and the moments you've reached, each
with its date: your first log, your 10th, 100th, 500th and 1,000th, your
first import, your first goal set and first goal reached, and one month,
three months and a year with Sorrel. Only the ones you've reached show up;
there's no list of things left to do.

A log is something you entered yourself or confirmed from the queue. An
import doesn't count toward them, and a transfer counts once. If you delete
entries, the list is re-read from what's there, so it can shrink.

When you reach one, a small toast says so, with the leaves, once on that
device, and "See all" opens the same panel. Nothing you've already reached
pops up when this arrives: it simply starts from where you are. None of it
is about amounts, and none of it is a comparison.

**What if.** "What if…" now reads "What if I spend…", on the dashboard and
in the runway. The answer is a sentence: "If you spend $300 on Saturday,
you'd still have $2,200 safe to spend." Same figure as before, in words.

**A footer.** On the public pages, and a slim row at the bottom of Settings
with Privacy, Terms and our TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. Those open in a
new tab. Sorrel doesn't load anything from them.
