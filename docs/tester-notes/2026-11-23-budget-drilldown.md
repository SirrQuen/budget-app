Budgets now open the spending behind them.

On Budgets, tap a category's name or its spent amount and you land on
your transactions for that category and month, with the total at the top
-- "Groceries · November 2026 · $412.50 spent". It's the same figure the
budget shows, and the list is exactly the transactions that add up to it.
This works for a budget carried over from an earlier month and for a $0
budget too; if nothing was spent, it says so. The back button takes you
to Budgets, and "Back to budgets" does the same.
