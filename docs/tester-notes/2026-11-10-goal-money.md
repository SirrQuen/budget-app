# Safe to spend now sets aside your goal money

Today's update: if a goal has a monthly target, safe to spend now sets
aside what's still to go into it this month.

What that means for your number:

- A goal with a $200 monthly target and nothing added yet this month
  takes $200 off safe to spend. Add $150 to it and only $50 comes off.
  Once you've hit the month's target, it takes nothing.
- It never takes more than a goal still needs. A goal that's $30 from
  done takes $30, even if its monthly target is bigger.
- Each goal counts on its own, so one goal that's ahead doesn't cover
  another that's behind.
- It's the same amount all month. It isn't spread across your days.
- It doesn't change when you'd run low. That's still your cushion alone,
  because goal money can wait if you need it to.

So if your safe to spend is lower today, this is probably why. Tap the
number on your dashboard and you'll see "Goals this month", with each
goal and its amount.

If a recurring transfer already moves money into a goal, link the two.
The goal form asks "Do you move this money automatically?", and the
transfer form asks "Is this for a goal?". Once they're linked, the
transfer counts and the goal doesn't, so nothing comes off twice.

Does the new number feel right to you? I'd like to hear either way,
especially if a goal is taking off more than you expected.
