New to Sorrel? Getting started now takes three short screens.

- **Your account.** Name the account you spend from, checking or cash, and
  enter what's in it today. No bank login and no account numbers.
- **Your pay.** Enter your take-home pay and your next payday, weekly,
  every 2 weeks, twice a month or monthly. If you're paid on the last day
  of the month, there's a choice for that. Sorrel asks each payday whether
  your pay landed; it never adds it on its own. If your pay isn't regular,
  you can skip this.
- **Your number.** Your first Safe to spend, with "See how we got there"
  to show the day-by-day runway behind it.

If you're already set up, nothing changes for you.
