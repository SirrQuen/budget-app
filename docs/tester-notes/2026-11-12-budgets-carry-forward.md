# Your budgets now carry from month to month

Today's update: a budget you set now stays set. Give Groceries $600 and
it's $600 next month too, and the month after, until you change it. On
1 December you won't need to enter your budgets again.

What that looks like:

- A budget that came forward says where from: "carried from November".
  That way an amount you haven't touched doesn't look like one you set
  for this month and forgot.
- Skipping a month doesn't reset anything. If you didn't touch your
  budgets in October, November still carries September's amounts.
- Changing an amount changes it from that month on. Set Groceries to $650
  in December and January carries $650. November keeps $600.
- Each category carries on its own, so changing one doesn't touch the
  others.

Two more things:

- **$0 is a budget.** If you set a category to $0 because you're not
  spending on it this month, it says "$0 budgeted, nothing spent" rather
  than looking unbudgeted.
- **"Stop budgeting" replaces Delete.** It stops a category's budget from
  the month you're looking at onwards. Earlier months keep theirs, and you
  can add one again any time. Delete used to bring back an older amount,
  which wasn't what anyone meant.

"Add budget" now adds to the month you're viewing. Use Prev and Next to
pick a different month first.

The "over budget most months" suggestion now counts carried months as
well. You may see it on a category where you didn't before.

Nothing you've set has changed. Your existing budgets are exactly as
they were; they just carry forward now.
