Income and Spending now open.

Tap Income or Spending on your dashboard and you land on the transactions
that make up that figure, for the same dates. At the top: the same total
as the tile, how many transactions it is, and where the money went (or
came from) by category. Tap a category to see just those. Transfers
between your own accounts aren't part of either figure, so they aren't in
the list. Search or filter and it shows how much of the total you're
looking at. Your back button returns you to the dashboard.
