petals around a crimson eye, glossy red calyces on the stems.

It opens with your morning (7:00) and closes with your evening (7:00pm),
in your own time zone. It plays that once, the first time you visit after
it changes; after that it just rests, open or closed. If you've asked your
device to reduce motion, it only ever rests. The red calyces are always
there, open or closed.

It's a little taller than the old flower, but it stands in the same space:
nothing on the dashboard moves down, and the number you lead with stays
where it was.
