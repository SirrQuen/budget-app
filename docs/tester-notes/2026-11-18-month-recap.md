At the start of each month, Sorrel now shows you a short look back at the
month before: a few cards you tap through, the first time you open the app
in the month's first week.

- **How many times you logged.** Everything you entered or confirmed that
  month. Imports aren't counted: bringing in a file isn't logging.
- **What you set aside for your goals**, with your biggest goal named and
  its meter as it stood at the month's end.
- **What you paid off a card**, if a debt goal follows it and the card
  owes less than it did when the month began.
- **What you have safe to spend right now.**

A card with nothing to say is left out, and if you logged fewer than five
times that month there's no look back at all. Tap Done or close it and it
won't come back until next month.

**Share** makes a picture of your month on your phone or computer -- it
isn't sent anywhere to be made. Amounts are hidden unless you switch on
"Show amounts", and you see the exact picture before you share it.

One thing it doesn't count yet: money moved to a goal by a recurring
transfer you confirm from the queue isn't linked to the goal, so it isn't
in "set aside". Linking those is coming.
