Upcoming can now be a timeline.

On the Upcoming card there's a new switch: List and Timeline. Timeline lays
the next 14 days out in a row, starting today. Each bill is a small pebble,
a paycheck landing in checking or cash is a sunrise, and the one day your
safe-to-spend figure calls your tightest gets a leaf.

Tap a day to see what's on it: each item with the account it comes from and
what its amount rests on, and a link to its schedule. On a phone the days
scroll sideways.

It shows the same items the list does, for the same 14 days, and nothing
about your figure changes. List is still what you see first; Sorrel
remembers your choice on that device.
