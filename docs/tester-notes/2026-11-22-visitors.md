A small visitor now drops by the dashboard.

The first time you open the dashboard in each part of the day, something
small flies in and lands beside your greeting: a songbird in the morning,
a butterfly in the afternoon, a firefly in the evening and an owl at night.
It arrives once, settles, and then sits still. Open the dashboard again in
the same part of the day and it's already there.

Tap it and it flies off, and it stays away until the next part of the day.
It never sits over a figure or a button, and if your screen is too narrow
for it to fit, it doesn't come. If your device is set to reduce motion, it
just sits there without moving.

Not for you? Settings, under Appearance, has "Visitors on the dashboard".
Turn it off and none will come. Your choice is kept on this device.
