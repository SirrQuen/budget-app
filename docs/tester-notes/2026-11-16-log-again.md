You can now log something again. On any transaction, in the Transactions
list or on its edit screen, choose **Log again**. It opens the add form
already filled in with the account, category, description and amount (a
transfer keeps both accounts), dated today. The amount is selected, so
typing a different one replaces it. Nothing is saved until you press Add
transaction.

Quick add also has a **Recent** row: your last few different
transactions from the past two months. Tap one to fill the form the same
way.

Notes, the date, and any link to a recurring schedule or an import aren't
copied. Nothing about your numbers changes.
