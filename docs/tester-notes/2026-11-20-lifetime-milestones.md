Sorrel now notices a few moments in your time with it, and keeps them.

Settings has a new section, Your Sorrel. It lists the ones you've reached,
each with its date: your first log, your 10th, 100th, 500th and 1,000th,
your first import, your first goal set and first goal reached, and one
month, three months and a year with Sorrel. Only the ones you've reached
show up; there's no list of things left to do.

A log is something you entered yourself or confirmed from the queue. An
import doesn't count toward them, and a transfer counts once.

When you reach one, a small toast says so, with the leaves, once on that
device. Nothing you've already reached will pop up when this arrives: it
simply starts from where you are.

None of it is about amounts, and none of it is a comparison. If you delete
entries, the list is re-read from what's there, so it can shrink.
