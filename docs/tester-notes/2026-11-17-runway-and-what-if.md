You can now see where your Safe to spend comes from, and ask about a
purchase before you make it.

- **See your runway.** Under Safe to spend, tap "See your runway" for your
  checking and cash balance drawn day by day through the end of the
  forecast. Each payday is a sun and each bill is a dip. A leaf marks your
  lowest point. One line shows the sum: your lowest point, minus your
  cushion, minus what's set aside for goals this month. Tap any sun or dip
  to open that paycheck or bill. Prefer a table? Tap "View as list".
- **What if…** Enter an amount and pick a day, and Sorrel shows what your
  Safe to spend would be and whether your tightest day moves. If it doesn't
  fit yet, Sorrel tells you the first day it would. Asking saves nothing.
  "Log it" opens the usual add form already filled in, and "Just looking"
  closes it.

The runway draws itself once each time you open the app. If your device is
set to reduce motion, it's simply there.
